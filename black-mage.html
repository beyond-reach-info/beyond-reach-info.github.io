How the fight works

  1. The fight has 4 phases. P1 is two knights, P2 and P3 are the Black Mage himself, and P4 is a solo fight where everyone is split into their own room.
  2. Each phase has a few FMAs (full map attacks). A warning banner shows up first, and your survival depends on reading it.
  3. After most FMAs the boss puts up a shield. Your hits drain the shield first, so the team saves origins for the right moments.
  4. You have 12 lives for the whole fight, personal and not shared. Lose them all and you can only watch.
  5. Most attacks give a white or black curse. Holding both at once is how lives disappear. That is the rule below.

P2 is the hardest stage, followed by P3. Practice them in Boss Practice Mode first (Dro).

The one rule: never hold both curses

Black · Destructive curse

  • More crit damage, but you take more damage
  • No potions, Power Elixirs or ACP while it's on (Fever tickets still work)
  • The team prefers to carry black, because black attacks are everywhere (like the chains from the top in P1 and P2)

White · Creative curse

  • You heal slowly, but deal less damage
  • You explode now and then, hurting players near you
  • Not the end of the world: if you pick up white and no black attacks are around, stay calm and dodge the black ones until it wears off
Curse Crossing: hit by the other color while cursed

You lose 1 life, take damage, and get skill locked. While locked you can't teleport, blink or i-frame, and ACP doesn't work. That's how one mistake snowballs into losing 4–5 lives. If it happens, stay calm and dodge with plain movement until the lock ends (Dro). Resurrect and Guardian can't block this, and at 0 lives you die on the spot.

Before you go

Prep and rewards

Bring
  • Potion cooldown is 10 sec (Power Elixir and All-Cure 5 sec)
  • Self-heals and i-frame skills, since black blocks potions
  • A teleport or i-frame for P1 Red Lightning
  • ACP stocked, and blink ready for P3 Blue Meteor
  • Practice in Boss Practice Mode (added in the Feb patch)
You get
  • 600 Sol Erda Energy every clear
  • After the kill, break the Genesis Crux in the reward map to get the loot
  • Chance at the Genesis Badge (5% Final DMG, 3% Boss DEF), or trade 60 Chain of Destruction + 20 Primal Essence for it
  • 1st clear: medal with 5% DEF Ignore, 1,000 ATK/MAG ATK
  • 4th clear: Source of Darkness mount
