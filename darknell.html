Darknell Run Guide

Compiled by Faye · Updated Oct 21, 2026

For reference only. Info may not be fully accurate and will keep being updated. Tap any picture to enlarge it.

Lv. 250+ 6 per guild team (max 10) 10 min 1 clear a week (resets Thu midnight) 10 revives for the whole team Aim for 70% DEF Ignore, 880 ARC
Nodes: use Withstand 2 unless you're the pin or an experienced DPS.

Everyone else runs Withstand 2. That's our standard.

One hit won't KO you. Combos will.

A single hit from the boss won't kill you. What kills you is combo death: getting hit by two things at the same time, like a light beam plus the blue ball, or a dash right into a meteor. Dodge so you're never caught by two at once.

Pick your run

Two kinds of guild runs

You'll be ready for each type at a different level. Start with Type 2.

Type 1 · harder

LK/Faye team

  • LK pins, Faye supports as Sia
  • No detailed calls. Commands cover the basics only
  • You need to know your own role and the general sequence. You don't have to memorize every step; we react to situations.
  • If Darknell teleports out on a dive, that's bad luck. Main team keeps DPS and waits for him to come back. Don't chase him.
Type 2 · easier

Dro commands

  • Dro pins and calls the whole sequence in detail
  • Comes with a bsp support
  • More room for error, good for learning
  • Calls include when the lure flies up to the left corner on a dive

8:08 is not a fixed time

It's just what we call it. The real moment is the first dive after the stun origin is back (~8:26 or later), so it can be ±5 sec or more depending on the run. Same for ~4:08. Wait for the dive and listen to command, not the clock.

  • Origin cooldown is 4 min. The stun cooldown is 1.5 min.
  • Chain stun: after each team origin, someone (usually the slot 5 support) counts 8 seconds and casts a stun. The real window is 10 sec, but if you're slow, Darknell wakes up before the chain.
  • After the opening, just wait for the call to origin together. Listen to command.

Where the main team stands

Only 3 people of space on the left. Never stand at the 4th spot or further.

Most DPS stand too far out in their first 3–4 runs and think the 4th spot is safe. It isn't. It's a strict, narrow area.

If the elites spot you and come to the left side, everyone can die, pin included. Even if you fly up and dodge, others die, pin dies out, and the run fails and needs a rerun.

If you need to avoid danger, don't sidestep a little to the right. Going further right is also OK.

What you're dodging
  • Light beam (Pillar of Light): a narrow beam shows first as a warning and is still safe. About 1 sec later it turns into a fat beam that hurts. Move when you see the narrow one.
  • Blue ball: one of the main things that kills you, in all 4 phases. Pictures below.
  • Dive: he flies up and smashes the ground. Fly up to dodge.
  • Meteors are for the blockers. Stay tucked in and keep hitting.
Role guide

Playing the lure

What the lure dodges
  • Blue cloud: Vicious Hunter's Summon Chains, a big blue area. Get out of it.
  • The elites' attacks: snipes, stuns and chains (all pictured under Attacks).
  • Plus the same light beams, blue balls and dives as everyone else.
Your job
  • About 0.5–1 min after the 8:08 origin, go to the right side to draw the elites away. Everyone else tucks in on the left.
  • On a dive, Darknell teleports to a random player. If it's you, fly up to the left corner to be with the team. In Type 2, Dro calls this.
  • Around the ~5 min and ~1 min team origins, jump left on the call, origin with the team, then jump back to lure.
Staying alive
  • Survival comes first. Use i-frames, walk, dash and teleport left and right, and fly up whenever you can.
  • Missing the dive timing is fine, like when dive comes while fly is on cooldown. Just survive.
  • Scared? Just fly up every time it's ready.
  • Want to register damage? You can come at most to the middle of the map. Never cross the middle. If you can't hit from there, don't hit.
If the lure dies

One of the DPS has to leave and take over lure. That's one less person hitting on the left, so the run gets slower and might fail.
